# Market-entry recommendations

> **Draft — Internal review only.** GTM-RESEARCH-01, 2026-11-10. An internal recommendation for owner review, based on [market data](telangana-andhra-pradesh.md), [segmentation](school-segmentation.md), the [competitor landscape](../competitors/competitor-landscape.md) and [positioning](../positioning-and-feature-gaps.md). **It authorises no outreach, campaign, price or product commitment.** Any contact with schools follows the [outreach controls](../../sales/outreach/privacy-and-outreach-controls.md) and needs the owner's go-ahead.

## Summary of the recommendation

| Question | Recommendation | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Segment to approach first | **Medium schools (501–1,000 students)**, starting in Telangana, for validation conversations and first pilots | Low–medium: reasoned from public evidence, untested with schools |
| Schools under 500 students | **Learn before selling**: include them in discovery conversations, but do not pursue sales until the fee decision (below) is made | Medium |

## 1. Which segment first, and why

**Medium schools (501–1,000) in Telangana**, for these reasons:

1. **Product fit is moderate rather than weak.** Medium schools have enough sections and teachers for assignment-based access, missing-register reports and controlled publication to matter (positioning H1, H4). Very small schools may feel these less.
2. **Competition at the bottom is price-led.** Free tiers and low monthly plans target small schools (S15, S21). Our setup-plus-support model is least competitive there (HYP).
3. **Telangana's private schools are larger on average and growing.** Average private enrolment is ≈374 in Telangana and ≈308 in Andhra Pradesh (DER). Telangana private schools grew by 771 (+6.2%) and enrolment by 4.2% between 2024-25 and 2025-26, against +1.5% and +2.9% in AP (GOV/DER, S01, S02).
4. **Some medium schools may already have a fee or accounts tool** and could keep it, reducing the impact of our largest gap (positioning H2). **This must be tested.**

**Not first: larger schools (1,001–2,000).** They are likely to need fees, integrations and possibly multi-campus support, all outside current scope. They may also expect references we do not have. Approach selectively once we have pilot experience (HYP).

**Not first, but learn from: small schools (300–500).** Average sizes suggest this band is commercially important, especially in AP. But current evidence points to fee collection and entry price as deciding factors, both unfavourable to our current offer. Use discovery conversations to test H2 and H3 before any sales effort.

## 2. Buyer roles to prioritise

| Role | Why | What they likely care about (HYP) |
|---|---|---|
| Owner / correspondent | Final decision and budget | Control, visibility, cost, reliability, support |
| Principal | Day-to-day academic authority; likely champion | Accurate attendance and results, controlled release to parents, teacher accountability |
| Office administrator | Daily user; data owner for migration | Ease of use, Excel import, fewer corrections and phone calls |
| Academic coordinator (larger schools) | Report cards and assessments | Grading rules, report formats, corrections |

Teachers and parents are users, not buyers (outreach kit).

## 3. Workflows to demonstrate first

Use the existing [8-minute executive demonstration](../../demo/presenter-scripts/executive-demo-8-minute.md), weighted towards:

1. **Attendance:** class-teacher register, and the office view of absentees and missing registers (M05 04, M05 06)
2. **Report-card publication control:** finalise, preview, release; guardians cannot see unpublished marks (M06 05–M06 08)
3. **Guardian access:** explicit per-child access, attendance and published report cards (M08)
4. **Excel import preview:** validation before anything is saved (M11)
5. **Teacher assignments:** dated responsibilities and the missing-assignment overview (M04), for schools with many sections

State the boundaries early (fees, automatic messaging, native app), as the demo script already requires.

## First-market validation plan

**Purpose:** test hypotheses H1–H5 ([positioning](../positioning-and-feature-gaps.md#5-testable-positioning-hypotheses)) and the segment choice with real schools. These are **learning conversations**, not sales calls.

**Status: proposed only. Do not start until the owner approves it and the pre-outreach checklist in the [controls](../../sales/outreach/privacy-and-outreach-controls.md#before-outreach-begins-owner-checklist) is complete.**

| Element | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Sample | About 12–15 schools: about 6–8 medium (501–1,000) in Telangana, 3–4 small (300–500) across Telangana and AP, 2–3 larger (1,001–2,000) |
| Who | Owner/correspondent or principal; office administrator where possible |
| How contacted | Only under the outreach controls: reasonable basis, manual messages, opt-outs respected, no scraping, attempt limit |
| Format | 20–30 minute conversation; optional 8-minute demo at the end |
| Records | Notes stored outside Git under the lead tracker rules; no student data; no school names in this repository |

**Questions to answer**

| Hypothesis | Key questions |
|---|---|
| H2 Fees | How are fees managed today? Would you adopt a system without fees? What tool do you use for fees? |
| H1 Results control | How do report cards reach parents? Any problems with errors or early release? |
| H4 Teacher responsibilities | How do you track who records attendance and marks? What happens when a teacher leaves mid-year? |
| H5 Messaging | How do you message parents today? Would staff-sent WhatsApp be acceptable? |
| H3 Model | Where do you expect your data to be kept? How do you prefer to pay for software: monthly, yearly, setup plus support? (No prices discussed) |
| Language | Which language do parents prefer for messages? |
| Competitors | Which systems have you used or seen? What did you like or dislike? (Record without criticising anyone) |

**Decision points after the conversations (proposed thresholds for owner review)**

- If most medium schools say they **cannot** adopt a system without fees, prioritise the fee decision before pursuing pilots.
- If several medium schools **can** keep fees elsewhere and respond positively to H1/H4, proceed to requirements discussions with them under the [sales process](../../sales/outreach/sales-process.md).
- If small schools consistently require fees and a free or monthly entry option, record the small-school segment as "not yet addressable" and revisit after product decisions.
- Revise [`target-segments.md`](../../sales/outreach/target-segments.md) with what was learned, without recording school names or quotes in Git.

## Risks

- The recommendation rests on public advertising and official statistics, not buyer evidence.
- Competitor features were not tested; vendors may be weaker or stronger than advertised.
- The fee-regulation context is unverified and must not be used externally without legal confirmation.
- Without approved identity, references or a trial, early conversations may be harder than competitors' (HYP).
