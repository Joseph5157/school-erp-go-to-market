# Executive summary: competitor and market research

> **Draft — Internal review only.** GTM-RESEARCH-01, research date 2026-11-10. For management. Based on official UDISE+ statistics and official vendor websites; no schools or vendors were contacted. Details and sources are in [`research/`](README.md).

## The market (official data)

- **Telangana has 13,245 and Andhra Pradesh 15,710 private unaided recognised schools** (UDISE+ 2025-26). Together they teach about 9.8 million pupils, which is **64.5% of enrolment in Telangana and 57.4% in Andhra Pradesh**.
- **Telangana's private sector is growing faster:** +771 schools (+6.2%) and +4.2% enrolment in one year, against +1.5% and +2.9% in Andhra Pradesh.
- **Private schools are small on average:** about 374 pupils per school in Telangana and 308 in Andhra Pradesh (derived). A large share may sit at or below the bottom of our 300–2,000 range.
- **Connectivity is reported as high:** 92.1% (TG) and 99.9% (AP) of private schools report an internet facility. Reliability is not measured.
- **No official count of private schools in our 300–500, 501–1,000 or 1,001–2,000 bands was found in the sources reviewed.** We have not estimated them. Only approximate, indicative all-school counts (derived from rounded percentages) are shown.

## The competition (vendor websites)

- **15 vendors found; 7 clearly marketed school-ERP vendors compared:** MyClassBoard (Hyderabad), Fedena, Entab, Campus 365, Vidyalaya, and two vendors targeting Telangana/AP specifically, Class ON App and ScholaBook (Hyderabad). **Teachmint** is profiled as an adjacent, unconfirmed competitor: its site now leads with classroom hardware and its current school ERP offering is not verified. It is excluded from the counts below.
- **All 7 advertise fee management and online payments.** 6 of 7 advertise native mobile apps and automatic SMS/WhatsApp. Some capabilities may be limited to particular plans or add-ons. **Our product offers none of these today.**
- **Pricing is mostly subscription.** Hosting is explicitly included at one vendor (Fedena); elsewhere hosting terms, and whether installations are shared or separate, are not publicly verified. Public prices exist for three vendors: Fedena ($999–$1,699 billed yearly; whether the "Web App" and "Web & Mobile App" selections cost the same was not established), Campus 365 Lite (₹5,999/month billed yearly, under 300 students) and ScholaBook (free up to 100 students; from ₹799/month). Others are demo- or quote-led.
- **Small schools are targeted with free or low-cost entry**, free trials and "no setup fee" offers.

## Where we stand

**Possible strengths (documented, not yet validated with schools):** controlled report-card release with corrections history; dated teacher assignments and accountability; explicit per-child guardian access; audited corrections; Excel import with a validation preview; a separate installation per school with written change control. Competitor pages did not describe these in comparable detail, but **that is not proof that they lack them**.

**Biggest gaps:** fee management and payments (decisive for many schools, likely for most small ones); native app and push; automatic messaging; transport, payroll and library; Telugu parent communication.

**Model risk:** our one-time setup fee plus separately shown hosting contrasts with free plans and with subscriptions that include or do not itemise hosting, especially for schools under 500 students (HYP).

## Recommendation (conditional, for owner decision)

1. **Leading hypothesis: start validation with medium schools (501–1,000) in Telangana**, where our academic workflows may matter most and price competition appears less extreme.
2. **Keep schools under 500 students in comparative discovery** alongside medium schools. Test whether they would adopt any system without fees, and how they react to a setup-plus-support model. Whether to pursue sales with them is an open owner decision.
3. **Decide the fee-management direction**, which remains undecided. Options include scoping fee management, partnering with a fee tool, or targeting schools that keep fees elsewhere. This research authorises no product development or pricing.
4. **Prepare new objection answers** for "free plan / monthly price elsewhere", "Telugu messages" and "how many schools use it".
5. **Run the proposed validation plan** of about 12–15 learning conversations, only after owner approval and under the outreach controls.

## Key uncertainties

- No buyer evidence yet: every positioning statement is a hypothesis
- Competitor capabilities are advertised, not tested
- Board mix, size distribution and software adoption among TG/AP private schools are unknown
- Fee-regulation status in both states is unverified
