## L1. Connected academic workflows

**Card headline:** From admission to report card, in one place.

**Post:**

> In many schools, a student's details are written down again and again: on the admission form, the class register, the marks sheet and the report card.
>
> Everclass ERP keeps these steps connected. The office reviews an application and admits the student. The student is placed in a class and section for the academic year. The class teacher records attendance against that class. Subject teachers enter marks. The office reviews results and decides when report cards are released. Guardians the school has approved can then see attendance and published report cards for their own children.
>
> Each school gets its own installation, set up with the school.

## L2. Attendance management

**Card headline:** Know which registers are still missing today.

**Post:**

> An absent student and a register that was never filled in are not the same thing, but on paper they can look alike.
>
> In Everclass ERP, the class teacher records Present or Absent for the class on the school day, can save a draft, and submits the complete register. The office can see the day's absentees and which sections have not submitted attendance. If a submitted register needs a correction, an administrator makes it, and the change is recorded.
>
> Attendance percentages are based on submitted records, and holidays and non-school days stay neutral.
>
> Contact [Your email] to see it with a sample school.

**Alt text:** Card reading "Know which registers are still missing today" with a simple list showing sections marked Submitted and Not submitted, each with a text label.

## L3. Assessment and report-card publication

**Card headline:** Report cards reach parents when the school decides.

**Post:**

> Marks entered by a teacher are not the same as results the school is ready to share.
>
> In Everclass ERP, teachers enter marks for the subjects they are assigned. A student marked absent is shown differently from a mark that is simply missing. The office finalises each assessment, and class teachers add term remarks. Before release, the office previews the term report cards and then publishes them explicitly.
>
> Guardians cannot see unpublished marks. Once released, they can view the report card and download the PDF.
>
> Contact [Your email] to arrange a demonstration.

**Alt text:** Illustration of report-card stages from "Not visible to guardians" to "Visible to guardians". Illustration only, not a product screenshot.

## L4. Teacher responsibilities

**Card headline:** Each teacher sees the classes they are responsible for.

**Post:**

> When responsibilities change mid-year, it should be clear who looks after which class and subject.

Teachers see the students, attendance and marks work for their own assignments, with their periods for the day. The office can see an assignment overview that flags classes or subjects without an active teacher.

## L5. Guardian access

**Card headline:** Parents see only their own children's information.

**Post:**

> Parents want to know about attendance and results. Schools need to be sure each parent sees only their own child.
>
> In Everclass ERP, the school creates the guardian's account, and the guardian sets a new password at first sign-in. The school grants access to each child explicitly, and one account can cover brothers and sisters where the school approves. Guardians can then see submitted attendance, published report cards and school notices relevant to their children.
>
> It works in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen on supported phones. An internet connection is needed.
>
> Contact [Your email] for a demonstration.

## L6. School onboarding and Excel import

**Card headline:** Check your spreadsheet before anything is saved.

**Post:**

> Moving to a new system often starts with a spreadsheet of students and parents.
>
> Everclass ERP provides an Excel template for students, guardians and class placement. When the file is uploaded, every row is checked first. Problems are listed by row and column, and possible duplicates or shared phone numbers are flagged for the school to review. Records are created only when the school confirms, and the whole batch is saved together.
>
> The school prepares the data it is authorised to share; we explain the checks and support the trial and final imports as agreed.
>
> Contact [Your email] to talk through onboarding.
