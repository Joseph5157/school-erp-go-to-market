## Purpose

Explain, in plain terms, what the product does for a private school, one workflow at a time. Leaders of private schools in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh are the audience. The aim is to start conversations that lead to a demonstration request, not to make claims.

## Channel rules

**LinkedIn**: post from an approved company page or an approved individual profile only, after owner sign-off. Do not tag schools or people without their permission. Do not send connection requests or direct messages in bulk.

**WhatsApp**: use the drafts only as:

- a WhatsApp Status update seen by people who already have our business number, or
- a one-to-one message to a contact who has asked to receive updates, after a conversation.

Never send them to broadcast lists, groups we do not run, or numbers we were not given for this purpose. Every one-to-one message keeps the opt-out line.

## Claims rules

Every post was written only from the [feature catalogue](../../docs/product/feature-catalogue.md), the [sales FAQ](../../sales/faq/sales-faq.md) and [brand tone rules](../../brand/brand-guidelines.md#7-tone-of-voice). Keep it that way:

- No testimonials, customer numbers, school names, "trusted by", ratings or awards
- No time or money savings, percentages or "guaranteed" results
- No claim of DPDP compliance, offline working, automatic WhatsApp/SMS/email sending, a native app, fee collection or multi-school use
- No prices, offers, discounts, delivery dates or service levels
- No screenshots of staging or of any real data; no credentials, sign-in links or staging URLs
- Re-verify each feature on the deployed demo build before a post is approved

## Visual guidance (Graphite & Institutional Green)

From [`brand/brand-guidelines.md`](../../brand/brand-guidelines.md). Visuals are not produced in this package. This section briefs whoever produces them.

| Element | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Background | Paper `#F5F6F3` or Surface `#FFFFFF` |
| Headline and body text | Graphite `#18212B`; secondary text Soft graphite `#4A5562` |
| Accent and call to action | Institutional green `#1F6B52` (strong `#185842`, wash `#E3EFE9`) |
| Cobalt `#1D5FA8` | Only for an "information" element, never as the main accent or call to action |
| Status colours | Only with a text label; never colour alone |
| Typeface | Noto Sans, regular and bold only; Noto Sans Telugu for any Telugu text (Telugu copy needs separate review) |
| Layout | One headline, one short supporting line, generous spacing on the 8 px grid, 10 px card radius, borders rather than heavy shadows |
| Logo | Placeholder area only until an approved logo exists; do not use the PWA icon as a logo |
| Imagery | Simple text cards or the existing draft diagram assets in [`diagrams/svg/`](../../diagrams/svg/) (for example `02_Student_Journey.svg`, `04_Report_Publication.svg`). No stock photos of children, no product screenshots unless they are fictional-data captures approved for publication |
| Sizes | LinkedIn 1200 × 1200 or 1080 × 1350 px; WhatsApp Status 1080 × 1920 px |
| Accessibility | Text contrast at least 4.5:1; add alt text for each LinkedIn image |
