# WhatsApp starter posts

> **Draft — Internal review only.** GTM-OUTREACH-01. Short versions of the [starter campaign](../campaigns/starter-campaign.md) posts. Not sent or scheduled.
>
> **Use only** as a WhatsApp Status update, or as a one-to-one message to a contact who has asked to receive updates. **Never** send to broadcast lists, groups or numbers we were not given for this purpose. See the [privacy and outreach controls](../../sales/outreach/privacy-and-outreach-controls.md).

When sending one-to-one, end every message with:

> *If you would prefer not to receive these updates, just reply "stop" and I will not send more.*

Status updates use the card image (1080 × 1920 px) and the caption only.
