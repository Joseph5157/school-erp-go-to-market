# Access reviews and sign-in lockouts

## Guardian access reviews

When a Student no longer has a current enrollment — for example after their Academic Year ends — School ERP stops showing that child's information to guardians until an administrator decides. Nothing is decided automatically.

The guardian sees **Access under review** for that child in the portal.

1. Go to *Access and security > Access reviews*.
2. Under **Pending reviews**, each row shows a student, a guardian and the student's last placement.
3. For each row, choose the decision — for example **Retain access** — and select **Record**.

**Recent decisions** lists what was decided, by whom and when.

A similar review opens automatically when you [deactivate a student](admissions-and-students.md#deactivate-or-reactivate-a-student) whose guardians have portal access.

## Sign-in lockouts

To protect accounts, School ERP refuses a username after repeated failed sign-ins: **5 from one network source, or 20 from any source, within 15 minutes**.

Lockouts expire on their own. To restore access sooner:

1. Go to *Access and security > Sign-in lockouts*.
2. Find the username and select **Clear**.
3. Select **Clear lockout** to confirm.

Clearing a lockout is recorded. Before clearing, confirm the request genuinely comes from the account holder.

## Security events and history

School ERP records important security and operational events, such as password resets, account changes, document issues and lockout clearances. Teacher pages show their recent security events; guardian pages show their account and access history.

## Good practice

- Give each person their own login. Never share accounts.
- Hand temporary passwords over in person. Never send them by message.
- Deactivate logins for staff who leave the school.
- Review **Access reviews** at the start of each Academic Year.
