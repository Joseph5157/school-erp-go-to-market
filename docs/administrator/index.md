# Administrator Manual

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

Administrators are the control point of School ERP. You configure the school, admit and place students, create teacher and guardian logins, review attendance and results, and decide what is released to guardians.

## Finding your way around

After you sign in, the **Overview** page shows what needs attention today and, for a new school, a setup checklist. The office sidebar is grouped as follows. You only see the items your permissions allow.

| Group | Pages |
|---|---|
| Home | **Overview** |
| School setup | **School**, **Academic Years**, **Classes/Grades**, **Subjects**, **Timetable**, **Teachers** |
| Admissions | **Applicants**, **Students**, **Guardians** |
| Daily | **Attendance**, **Attendance reports**, **Announcements**, **Message queue**, **Documents** |
| Data tools | **Import** |
| Assessments & Results | **Assessments**, **Subject/Section results**, **Grading scales**, **Stale publications** |
| Access and security | **Access reviews**, **Sign-in lockouts** |

Each **Academic Year** card under *School setup > Academic Years* also links to that year's **Calendar**, **Teaching** pages (subjects offered, class teachers, teacher assignments) and **Assessment** pages (terms, assessments, grading scales).

Breadcrumbs at the top of each page show where you are. A number next to a sidebar item shows work waiting there.

## Principles to keep in mind

- **Nothing reaches guardians by accident.** Saving or finalising teacher work does not publish it. Report cards are released only when you publish them.
- **History is kept.** Corrections to attendance, results and student details need a reason and keep the original record. Assignments that have started are ended, not deleted.
- **Access is explicit.** A guardian record, or a relationship to a student, does not give portal access by itself. You grant access per child.
- **Permissions decide what you see.** Pages and buttons appear only if your account is allowed to use them. If something described here is missing, ask the person who manages staff permissions.

!!! warning "Protect personal data"
    Do not show temporary passwords, credential slips or student personal data on shared screens, in screenshots or in messages outside the school's approved process.
