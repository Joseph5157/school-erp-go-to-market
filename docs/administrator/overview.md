# Administrator Overview

Status: **Draft**. Verify against the deployed demonstration build before client publication.

## Purpose

Administrators configure the school structure, manage records and accounts, supervise daily workflows, review outputs and control what is released to guardians.

## Main areas

- School profile, academic years, classes, sections, subjects, terms and calendar
- Admissions decisions, student creation, enrolments and placement history
- Teacher and guardian accounts with role and permission boundaries
- Class-teacher and subject-teacher assignments with effective dates
- Attendance review, missing-register reporting and authorised corrections
- Assessment setup, completeness review, finalisation and report-card publication
- Timetable maintenance and clash review
- Announcements, WhatsApp Assist preparation and supported school PDFs
- Excel onboarding preview, validation and controlled commit
- Security events and traceable operational history

## Access boundaries

- The interface shows only actions allowed by the administrator's permissions.
- High-impact changes use explicit workflows and preserve relevant history.
- Temporary credentials and secrets must never appear in brochures, slides or shared screens.
- Fees, online payments, automatic messaging, transport, library, hostel, payroll and inventory are not current standard modules.

## Demo emphasis

Show the administrator as the control point: configure, review, approve, publish and audit. Do not imply that saving teacher work automatically releases information to guardians.
