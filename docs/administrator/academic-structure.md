# Classes, sections, subjects and terms

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

The academic structure has four parts:

| Part | Belongs to | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Class/Grade and its Sections | The whole school | *Grade 5*, Sections *A* and *B* |
| Subject | The whole school | *Mathematics* (code *MATH*) |
| Subject offered | One Academic Year and one Class/Grade | Mathematics for Grade 5 in 2026–27 |
| Term | One Academic Year | *Term 1*, 1 June to 30 September |

## Classes/Grades and Sections

### Create a Class/Grade

1. Go to *School setup > Classes/Grades*.
2. Select **Create class/grade**.
3. Enter the name, for example *Nursery*, *LKG* or *Grade 1*.
4. Select **Save class/grade**.

To change the order in which classes appear, select **Change order**, use the arrows, then **Save order**.

### Add Sections

1. On *Classes/Grades*, open the Class/Grade.
2. Select **Create section**, enter the name (for example *A*), and select **Save section**.

Use **Rename** to correct a Class/Grade or Section name.

### Archive or restore

When a Class/Grade or Section is no longer used, archive it instead of deleting it. An archived record cannot be picked for anything new, and everything already recorded against it is kept.

1. Open the Class/Grade and select **Archive this Class/Grade**, or select **Archive** next to a Section.
2. If it is still in use, Everclass ERP lists what must be moved or ended first, such as current placements or assignments.
3. When nothing is in use, confirm the archive.

To bring it back, open it and select **Restore this Class/Grade** or **Restore section**.

## Subjects

Subjects belong to the whole school. Each Academic Year then offers them to Classes/Grades.

1. Go to *School setup > Subjects* and select **Add subject**.
2. Enter the name and an optional short code such as *MATH* (codes are stored in capitals).
3. Leave **Active** ticked and select **Save subject**.

Renaming a Subject changes draft and future assessments only. Finalised results keep the name they were finalised with.

## Subjects offered in an Academic Year

1. Go to *School setup > Academic Years* and, on the year's card, open **Subjects offered** under **Teaching**.
2. Select **Offer a Subject**.
3. Choose the **Class/Grade** and the **Subject**.
4. Under Sections, leave every box unticked to offer the Subject to every Section of the Class/Grade, or tick only the Sections that take it.
5. Set the **Report card position**, which controls where the Subject appears on that Class/Grade's report cards.
6. Select **Save subject offering**.

To reorder subjects for report cards, select **Change order**, move the subjects, then **Save order**. Report cards already published do not change.

Use **Which Subjects does a Section take?** at the bottom of the page for a read-only check.

## Terms

Terms divide the Academic Year. Report cards and class-teacher remarks are written per Term.

1. On the year's card, open **Terms** under **Assessment**.
2. Select **Add term**.
3. Enter the name, start date, end date and order.
4. Select **Save term**.

Terms are shown in their explicit order and may not overlap. Each Term row also links to **Publish to guardians** and **Remarks** for that Term; see [Assessments and report cards](assessments-and-report-cards.md).
