# Signing in and passwords

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

Everyone uses the same **Log in** page. What you see after signing in depends on your role: the office pages for administrators, **My day** for teachers, and the **Guardian Portal** for guardians.

## Sign in

1. Open the School ERP address your school gave you.
2. Enter the **Username** the school gave you. Usernames are not case-sensitive.
3. Enter your **Password**. Select **Show** if you want to check what you typed.
4. Select **Log in**.

If the username or password is wrong, the page shows a general error message. For security, it does not say which of the two was wrong.

## First sign-in: change your temporary password

New teacher and guardian logins are created with a **temporary password**, printed on a credential slip by the school office. The first time you sign in, School ERP shows **Change your password** and you must choose a new password before you continue.

1. Enter the temporary password as your old password.
2. Enter a new password twice. The page checks it as you type. A new password must:
    - have the minimum number of characters shown on the page,
    - not be only numbers,
    - not be similar to your username,
    - not be a common password such as "password123" or the school name.
3. Select **Change password**.

If you do not want to continue, select **Log out instead**.

!!! tip "Choose a password you don't use anywhere else"
    Never share your password, even with school staff. The school office can reset it if needed.

## Forgotten password

There is no self-service reset. Ask the school office. An administrator can reset your password and give you a new temporary password, which you must change at your next sign-in.

## Too many failed attempts

After repeated failed sign-ins, the username is refused for a short time. The lockout ends on its own. If you need access sooner, ask the school office: an administrator can clear the lockout from **Sign-in lockouts** (see [Access reviews and sign-in lockouts](../administrator/access-and-security.md)).

## Staying signed in

For security, School ERP signs you out automatically:

- after about **30 minutes** without activity, and
- after about **8 hours** in total, even if you are active.

Unsaved work on an open page may be lost when this happens, so save drafts regularly. Changing your password or having your account changed by the office can also sign you out of other devices.

## If you have more than one role

Some staff members have both office and teacher responsibilities. School ERP may ask you to **choose where to go** after signing in. Each area shows only the actions your permissions allow.

## Installing on a phone

On supported phones and browsers, School ERP can be added to the home screen like an app. It still needs an internet connection: there is no offline mode and no push notifications.
