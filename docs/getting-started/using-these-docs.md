## Conventions

- **Bold text** is the exact name of a page, menu item or button on screen, for example **Save draft**.
- *Menu > Page* describes where to find a page in the office sidebar, for example *School setup > Academic Years*.
- Steps are numbered in the order you carry them out.
- Notes in blue give information; green tips suggest good practice; amber warnings explain something that cannot easily be undone.

## Terms used in Everclass ERP

Academic Year
:   The school year, with a start and end date. Terms, subjects offered, class teachers, subject teachers, timetables and assessments all belong to one Academic Year.

Class/Grade and Section
:   A Class/Grade (for example *Grade 5*) holds one or more Sections (for example *A* and *B*). Students are placed in a Section.

Term
:   A part of the Academic Year, such as *Term 1*. Report cards and class-teacher remarks are written per Term.

Applicant
:   A child who has applied for admission. An accepted Applicant is progressed into a Student record.

Placement (enrollment)
:   A Student's dated place in a Class/Grade and Section for an Academic Year.

Guardian
:   A parent or carer linked to a Student. Recording a Guardian does **not** give them portal access.

Register
:   The attendance record for one Section on one school day. It is a **draft** until it is **submitted**.

Finalise
:   An administrator makes an assessment's results official. Finalising does **not** publish anything to guardians.

Publish
:   An administrator releases a Term report card to guardians.

## Searching

Select the search box at the top of the page, or press ++ctrl+k++ or ++slash++, and type a word or phrase. Results show matching pages and headings. Useful searches include *register*, *absent*, *marks*, *finalise*, *publish*, *report card*, *guardian*, *placement*, *timetable*, *notice*, *bonafide* and *import*.

!!! note "Spelling"
    The product uses US spellings on some buttons, such as **Finalize results**. These manuals quote buttons exactly as they appear and otherwise use Indian English.
