# Guardian Overview

Status: **Draft**. Verify against the deployed demonstration build before client publication.

## Purpose

The Guardian Portal gives an authorised guardian access to eligible information for their approved child relationships.

## Main areas

- Approved child switcher when access exists for more than one child
- Child profile and current placement information
- Submitted attendance summary and monthly attendance view
- Published term report cards and eligible report-card PDF download
- School notices relevant to the child's current audience

## Access boundaries

- Access requires an approved guardian relationship and explicit portal grant.
- A guardian sees only children for whom access is currently authorised.
- Unpublished teacher marks and unrestricted administration pages are not exposed.
- Access is rechecked when relationships, placements or student status change.
- WhatsApp Assist remains staff-completed; it is not automatic provider delivery.

## Demo emphasis

Switch from the administrator or teacher profile to a separate guardian browser profile. Confirm the fictional child's identity, then show attendance, a published report card and a relevant notice.
