# Guardian Manual

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

The **Guardian Portal** lets you see school information about your own children, after the school has given you access.

## What you can see

For each child the school has given you access to:

- your child's name, admission number, class, section, roll number and enrolment status;
- attendance the school has officially recorded, by month and academic year;
- term report cards the school has published, and their PDFs;
- school notices for your child's class or for the whole school.

You cannot see other children's information, marks that the school has not published, or school office pages.

## Portal menu

| Menu item | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Home** | All your children and the latest notices |
| **Notices** | All school notices for you |
| **Overview** | The selected child's school details |
| **Attendance** | The selected child's attendance calendar |
| **Report cards** | The selected child's published report cards |

On a phone, the menu appears as tabs at the bottom of the screen.
