# Attendance

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

The **Attendance** page shows attendance the school has officially recorded for your child.

## Open attendance

1. Choose your child (see [Viewing your children](children.md)).
2. Select **Attendance** in the menu, or **View attendance** on the child's overview.

## Reading the page

At the top is a summary for the academic year:

- the **percentage** present;
- **Present** — days your child was marked present;
- **Absent** — days your child was marked absent;
- **Recorded days** — school days with officially recorded attendance.

To look at another year, choose the **Academic Year** and select **Show**.

### The monthly calendar

Each school day shows one symbol:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ | Present |
| ✕ | Absent |
| – | Not recorded |

Use **← Previous month** and **Next month →** to move between months.

!!! info "“Not recorded” does not mean absent"
    *Not recorded* means the school has not recorded attendance for that school day. It does not mean your child was absent. Days with no mark at all are not school days for your child, or have not happened yet.

## Questions about attendance

If you think a day is recorded wrongly, contact your child's class teacher or the school office. Only the school office can correct recorded attendance.
