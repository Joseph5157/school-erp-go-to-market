# Report cards and PDFs

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

You can see a term report card only after the school has **published** it. Marks that teachers have entered but the school has not yet published are not shown.

## View report cards

1. Choose your child (see [Viewing your children](children.md)).
2. Select **Report cards** in the menu (**Reports** on a phone).
3. The list shows each published report card with its term, academic year and class. Select one to open it.

If nothing has been published, the page says *No report cards have been published yet.*

## Reading a report card

For each subject, the report card shows every assessment separately with:

- **Marks** — for example *42 / 50*, or **Absent**;
- **%** — the percentage;
- **Grade**;
- **Result** — pass or fail, where the school uses a pass mark.

At the end you may see the **Class teacher's remark**.

There are no totals, weighting or ranks. If the report card says *Some results for this Term are not yet published*, the school released the results that were ready and will publish the rest later.

## Download the PDF

On the report card, select **Download PDF**. You can save or print the PDF.

!!! note
    If the school corrects a result after publishing, the report card may disappear briefly until the school reviews and publishes the corrected version.

## Questions about results

Contact the class teacher or the school office. The school office makes any corrections.
