# Account access

## Getting a login

The school creates your Guardian Portal login. You cannot register yourself.

1. The school office confirms your identity, for example in person.
2. The office gives you a printed slip with your **username** and a **temporary password**. The school will not send your password by message.
3. The office gives you access to each of your children separately.

## First sign-in

1. Open the Everclass ERP address your school gave you.
2. Enter your **Username** and the temporary **Password**, and select **Log in**.
3. On **Change your password**, enter the temporary password and then a new password twice.
4. Select **Change password**.

Your new password must meet the rules shown on the page. Choose a password you do not use anywhere else.

## Signing in later

Use your username and the password you chose. For security you are signed out after about 30 minutes without activity, or after about 8 hours.

## Problems signing in

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Forgot password | Ask the school office to reset it. You will get a new temporary password. |
| Username refused after several attempts | Wait and try again later, or ask the school office. |
| Signed in but a child is missing | Ask the school office to give you access to that child. |
| A child shows **Access under review** | The school is reviewing access, for example after a change of class or the end of an academic year. Contact the office. |

## Keep your account safe

- Do not share your username or password, including with family members. Each guardian can have their own login.
- Sign out on shared phones or computers.
- On supported phones you can add the portal to your home screen. It still needs an internet connection.
