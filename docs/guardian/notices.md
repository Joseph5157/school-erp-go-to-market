# School notices

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

The school publishes notices for the whole school, one class or one section. You see notices for the classes and sections of the children you have access to.

## Read notices

1. Select **Notices** in the menu, or **All notices** on **Home**.
2. The list shows each notice's title and date, newest first.
3. Select a notice to read it in full.

**Home** also shows the **Latest notices**.

## Notices that disappear

If the school withdraws a notice, for example to correct a mistake, it no longer appears in the portal. The school may publish a corrected notice in its place.

## Notices on WhatsApp

If your school uses WhatsApp Assist and you have agreed to receive WhatsApp messages, school staff may also send you notices on WhatsApp from their phone. The Guardian Portal remains the official place to read them.
