# Viewing your children

## Home

After signing in you see **Home** with **Your students**: one card for each child you can view. Each card shows the child's class and section, and:

- **Attendance** — the percentage of recorded school days present, or *No attendance recorded yet*;
- **Latest report card** — or *No report cards published yet*.

Select **Open overview** on a card to see that child's details.

Below the cards, **Latest notices** shows recent school notices. Select **All notices** to see the full list.

## A child's overview

The **Overview** page shows your child's **School details**:

- Admission number
- Academic Year
- Class/Grade and Section
- Roll number
- Enrolment status

If your child has no current class, it says **No current placement**.

## Switching between children

If you have access to more than one child:

1. At the top of the page, next to **Viewing**, select **Switch**.
2. Choose the child.

The portal keeps you on the same kind of page, so if you were looking at one child's attendance you will see the other child's attendance.

## If a child is missing or under review

- A child appears only after the school gives you access to that child.
- **Access under review** means the school is checking your access, for example at the end of an academic year. No information is shown until the school decides. Contact the school office if you have questions.
