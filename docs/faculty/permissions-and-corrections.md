# Permissions and corrections

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

## What you can do

| Work | Who | Until when |
|---|---|---|
| Take attendance for a Section | Its class teacher | Today only, until submitted |
| Enter assessment marks | The assigned subject teacher | Until the office finalises the assessment |
| Write term remarks | The class teacher | Any time; changes after publication make the report card stale |
| View students and results | Teachers of the Section or Subject | While the assignment is in force (past duties are read-only) |

## What you cannot do

- Open the office pages, such as admissions, accounts, school settings or publishing.
- Change a **submitted** attendance register.
- Change a **finalised** assessment result.
- Publish report cards to guardians.
- See Sections or Subjects you are not assigned to.

Pages you are not allowed to open show an access-denied message.

## Asking for a correction

If submitted attendance or a finalised mark is wrong:

1. Tell the school office which student, Section, date or assessment is affected, and what the correct value is.
2. An administrator records the correction **with a reason**. The original value, the person who corrected it and the time are kept permanently.
3. If a published report card is affected, the office reviews and republishes it.

## Why the rules exist

- **Blank is not absent and not zero.** School ERP never guesses a result for a student you have not marked.
- **History is preserved.** Corrections are added alongside the original instead of overwriting it, so the school can always explain a change.
- **Guardians see only what the school publishes.** Nothing you save is shown to guardians until the office publishes it.
