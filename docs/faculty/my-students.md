# My students

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

**My students** lists the students enrolled today in Sections where you are the class teacher or teach a Subject.

## Find a student

1. Select the **My students** tab (**Students** on a phone).
2. Students are grouped by Section. Your own class is marked **Class teacher**.
3. Each Section shows today's attendance: each student's Present or Absent status, or *Today's attendance not submitted yet.*
4. Select a student's name to open their page.

## A student's page

The page shows:

- the student's Section and **Admission number**;
- **Attendance this year** — days present and absent, and the percentage present, from submitted registers;
- **Results** — finalised results you are allowed to see, with subject, assessment, date, score, percentage, grade and pass/fail;
- **Guardians** — name, relationship, phone and email, where your permissions allow.

Select **All my students** to go back.

!!! warning "Use guardian contact details responsibly"
    Use guardian phone numbers and email addresses only for school purposes and according to school policy. Do not copy them into personal lists or groups.

## Results for my Subjects

The **Results** tab shows finalised results for the Subjects and Sections you teach:

- **Current duties** — Subjects you teach now;
- **Starting soon** — assignments that start later;
- **Past duties** — read-only; only results from the dates you taught each Subject are shown.

Open a Subject, optionally choose a **Term**, and select **Show** to see one row per student and one column per assessment. "Absent" is never counted as zero, and a dash means no result is recorded.
