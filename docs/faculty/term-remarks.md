# Class-teacher term remarks

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

As class teacher you can write one optional remark per student for each Term. The remark appears on the student's Term report card. Remarks never affect marks or grades.

## Write remarks

1. Open the Term remarks page from either:
    - **My day**, under **Term remarks**, or
    - **My assessments**, under **Term remarks**.
2. If you are class teacher of more than one Section, choose the **Section** and select **Open**.
3. Type a remark in the box beside each student. The **Last written by** column shows who last changed each remark.
4. Select **Save remarks**.

To remove a remark, clear its box and select **Save remarks**.

!!! tip "Writing good remarks"
    Keep remarks short, specific and respectful. Describe progress and next steps. Remember that guardians will read them once the report card is published.

## When guardians see remarks

Remarks are released only when the school office **publishes** the Term report card. If a remark is changed after publication, the report card becomes stale and guardians stop seeing it until the office reviews and republishes it.

## If the Section is missing

The remarks page lists only Sections you are class teacher of. If your Section is missing, ask the school office to check your class-teacher assignment.
