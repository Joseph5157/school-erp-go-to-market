# Entering assessment marks

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

Subject teachers enter marks for the assessments the school office has planned for their Subject and Section.

## Open the gradebook

1. Select the **My assessments** tab.
2. Each Subject and Section you teach is listed. Select **Gradebook**.
3. The gradebook shows every student and every assessment. An empty cell means *not entered yet*, not absent. Draft absences are labelled *(draft)*.

If the tab says **No assessments assigned**, no Subject is assigned to you at present. Ask the school office.

## Enter marks for an assessment

1. From the gradebook, open the assessment.
2. The header shows the Subject, Section, **out of** (maximum marks) and the pass mark, if any.
3. For each student, enter a **score**, or choose **Absent**.
4. Leave a row **blank** if you have not marked that student yet. Blank is not absent.
5. Select **Save draft**.

Use the filters **All**, **Not entered**, **Marked** and **Absent** to check progress. The counts show how many students are in each state.

!!! info "Marks entry opens on the assessment date"
    Before the assessment date the page says *Marks entry opens on* that date.

## What happens next

1. You can return and change draft marks until the assessment is finalised.
2. An **administrator finalises** the results once every student has a score or Absent. Finalised results become official.
3. Finalising does not show anything to guardians. The office **publishes** Term report cards separately.

Once finalised, the page says *This assessment is finalized. Ask an administrator if a result needs correcting.*

## Viewing finalised results

Use the **Results** tab to see finalised results for your Subjects and Sections. See [My students](my-students.md#results-for-my-subjects).
