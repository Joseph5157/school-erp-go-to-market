# Teacher Manual

<p class="erp-draft" data-search-exclude><strong>Draft.</strong> Verify against the deployed build before sharing.</p>

School ERP gives teachers a focused workspace for the classes, students and subjects assigned to them by the school office.

## Your four tabs

On a phone, the tabs appear at the bottom of the screen with short names. On a computer they appear at the top.

| Tab | Phone label | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **My day** | Today | Today's attendance, timetable, assignments and pending term remarks |
| **My students** | Students | Students in the Sections you teach today |
| **My assessments** | Assessments | Gradebooks, marks entry and term remarks |
| **Results** | Results | Finalised results for the Subjects you teach |

## What you can do depends on your assignments

- As a **class teacher** of a Section, you take its daily attendance and write term remarks.
- As a **subject teacher** of a Subject in a Section, you enter assessment marks for it.
- Assignments have start and end dates. You see a Section only while your assignment is in force.

If a class or subject is missing, ask the school office: assignments are set by administrators.
