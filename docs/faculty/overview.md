# Faculty / Teacher Overview

Status: **Draft**. Verify against the deployed demonstration build before client publication.

## Purpose

Faculty use a focused workspace for the classes, students, subjects and academic tasks assigned to them.

## Main areas

- Today's assigned work and configured timetable periods
- My Students within current effective assignments
- Daily class attendance with Present, Absent and draft/submitted states
- Gradebook and assessment marks for assigned subjects and sections
- Explicit distinction between Marked, Absent and not-yet-recorded results
- Student academic views permitted by the teacher's assignment
- Class-teacher term remarks where the school uses them

## Access boundaries

- Authority follows the teacher's effective class and subject assignments and academic year.
- Teachers do not receive unrestricted office-administration access.
- A blank attendance or mark is not silently converted into absence or zero.
- Submitted or finalised work follows the authorised correction workflow.
- Guardian contact and report-card access remain limited by role and year-specific authority.

## Demo emphasis

Show one coherent path: open Today, take attendance, enter assessment results and explain how the office reviews and publishes the final information.
