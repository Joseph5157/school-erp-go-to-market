# School ERP — Eight-Minute Executive Demonstration

**Status: Draft.** Audience: school proprietor, principal, administrator and academic coordinator. This is a **read-only**, short alternative to the [30-minute workflow script](live-demo-30-minute.md), not a replacement. All displayed school and student records must be fictional.

**Before speaking:** complete the [pre-demo checklist](../demo-checklists/pre-demo-readiness.md). Use the actual verified staging records. The 26 October 2026 staging snapshot (110 students, 10 teachers, Term 1, school logo, timetable and announcements) is a historical reference, not a promise of the present build. Never show account credentials.

## Operator cues

- On desktop, the teacher landing page is **My day** (labelled **Today** on phones). Use three separate authenticated test browser profiles. Switch between Admin, Teacher and Guardian without displaying login or credential screens.
- Select **verified existing** records; do not assume “Aarav Demo / Grade 6 A” or any exact prepared assessment exists in the current demo.
- Avoid writes in this shorter presentation. If a prepared example is unavailable, say so and substitute a rehearsed fictional screenshot or diagram.
- Rehearse with a **10-minute meeting slot** so transitions have breathing room; if strictly limited to 8 minutes, skip the Excel preview and show the module map instead.
- Pause after the meeting's top-priority question; record detailed needs privately, not in Git.

## Clear boundaries to state if asked

- Current PWA: installable on supported devices, **online-only**; no offline sync or native app promised.
- WhatsApp Assist: staff completes sends; **not** automatic provider delivery.
- Fees and online payments, transfer certificates, bulk academic-year rollover, native apps, offline workflows and multi-school SaaS need **separate assessment or future implementation**.
- Logo displayed in the signed-in app does not imply logo printing on PDFs.
- Telugu fonts in PDFs do not mean an existing full Telugu UI.
- Pricing: customized installation, setup fee plus annual support; **no amount or delivery date** until requirements and responsibilities are approved.

## Closing handoff

Ask who approves scope, who owns data collection and who will coordinate acceptance testing. Agree a private follow-up channel and provide the school requirements capture sheet. Do not promise any unverified capability or use a real school's student data to illustrate it.
