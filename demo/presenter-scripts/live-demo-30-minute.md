# School ERP Live Demonstration Script

Status: **Draft**. Thirty-minute presenter script for a rehearsed demo using fictional data only.

For the company presenter and demo operator.

Use the quoted paragraphs as your spoken script. Follow the screen actions between them. The sequence takes about 30 minutes including navigation and a short requirements discussion. Replace the company and product placeholders before the meeting.

## Preparation before the client joins

| **Prepare**    | **Required state**                                                                                                                        |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Accounts       | Separate Administrator, Teacher and Guardian browser profiles. Complete initial password changes before the meeting.                      |
| Student story  | Aarav Demo, enrolled in Grade 6 A, with a linked guardian and accessible original application.                                            |
| Attendance     | A small section of three fictional students. Today must be an eligible school day and its register must be ready for the intended action. |
| Assessment     | Unit Test 1 Mathematics, maximum 25. Teacher assignment must cover the assessment date. Prepare a complete sample roster.                 |
| Publication    | Prepare the term data so the intended publication preview can pass. Keep a separate completed report card as a fallback.                  |
| Other examples | One relevant notice, a weekly timetable, an import preview with a sample issue, and supported sample PDFs.                                |
| Communication  | Fictional contacts only. Keep real recipients out of all demo records. Do not send a real WhatsApp message during the demonstration.      |

Team roles One presenter speaks while one operator navigates, if two people are available. With one person, pause after each explanation before clicking. Keep credentials out of the shared screen.

# Opening and school administration

## Minutes 0 to 2 Welcome

**On screen** Open the Admin Overview. Keep the student list and Teacher and Guardian profiles ready in their separate browser sessions.

**Say** “Good morning, and thank you for your time. Today I will demonstrate our School ERP. We will follow a sample student through admissions, attendance and results, then see what the teacher and guardian can access.”

**Say** “This demonstration uses fictional school records. After the walkthrough, we would like to understand your school process and identify the configuration or additional work you need.”

## Minutes 2 to 4 Dashboard and academic setup

**On screen** Point to the school name, selected academic year, school statistics and attention items. Briefly open the academic setup for classes, sections, subjects and calendar. Return to Overview.

**Say** “The office starts here. The overview shows school information and work that needs attention. We can open the relevant records from this area. The school sets up its academic years, classes, sections, subjects and working calendar before daily operations begin.”

**Say** “Records remain associated with their academic year. This lets staff inspect earlier records without replacing them when the next year begins.”

## Minutes 4 to 7 Admissions and student profile

**On screen** Open Aarav Demo's prepared applicant record. Show guardian details and the admission decision. Open his existing student profile, enrollment, admission number and roll number.

**Say** “This is Aarav's original application. The school records an admission decision first. Once an applicant is accepted, an authorized staff member can create the student record and enroll that student in the correct year, class and section.”

**Say** “Here is Aarav's student profile. It brings together his placement and guardian relationships. His admission number identifies the student, while his roll number belongs to the enrollment. Earlier placement history stays available.”

*Presenter note Use the prepared history for the main demo. If the client asks to see live creation, use a separate unused applicant and allow extra time. Do not recreate an existing student. Pause briefly for an admissions question, then continue.*

# Teacher access and attendance

## Minutes 7 to 8 Teacher view

**On screen** Switch to the Teacher browser profile. Open Today. Point to the assigned class, relevant academic work and today's periods when configured.

**Say** “I will now switch to the teacher account. Teachers have their own area for the work assigned to them. They can see their students and academic tasks within those assignments. When a timetable is configured, their own periods appear on Today.”

## Minutes 8 to 10 Record the class register

**On screen** Open attendance for Grade 6 A. Confirm the school date and roster. In the prepared small section, mark Aarav Present, another fictional student Absent and the remaining student Present. Save and submit the complete register if submission is available.

**Say** “The class teacher opens today's register. The students shown belong to this section on this date. I will mark Aarav Present and use another sample student to show an absence. An absence can include a note.”

**Say** “The register stays in draft while attendance is incomplete. Submission confirms the complete roster. A blank or unsubmitted record does not become an absence.”

## Minutes 10 to 11 Review and reporting

**On screen** Switch to the Administrator profile. Open the submitted register, student attendance history or monthly summary. Point to the correction action without changing the submitted example.

**Say** “Office staff can review submitted registers, absentees, missing attendance and monthly summaries. When a submitted record needs correction, an authorized staff member uses the correction workflow so the previous value remains traceable.”

*Presenter note Teachers submit today's attendance only within their permitted assignment. If today is a holiday or the sample register is already submitted, show the prepared submitted example and explain its state. Do not change the server date or imply that the teacher can reopen a submitted register.*

## Transition to assessments

**Say** “We have now recorded the student's attendance. Next, we will follow the marks entry and report card release process.”

# Assessments and report card release

## Minutes 11 to 13 Teacher marks entry

**On screen** Switch to the Teacher profile. Open the prepared Mathematics assessment or gradebook. Show the assessment date, maximum marks and roster. Enter or inspect a sample mark of 20 out of 25 for Aarav. Complete all required sample results before the office finalization step.

**Say** “The teacher opens an assessment for an assigned subject and section. This example is Mathematics Unit Test 1, with a maximum of 25 marks. Aarav has scored 20. The teacher can record a mark or mark a student Absent.”

**Say** “Absent and missing results have different meanings. We keep that distinction so an unfinished entry does not silently become an absence or a zero.”

## Minutes 13 to 15 Office finalization

**On screen** Switch to the Administrator profile. Open the same assessment, review completeness, and finalize the prepared complete roster. Then open the result sheet or term report card.

**Say** “The office reviews the assessment before finalizing it. Finalization records the completed results and the grading scale used. Any later change follows the authorized correction workflow.”

**Say** “The school can inspect assessment sheets, student result history and term report cards. Class teacher remarks can be included where the school uses them.”

## Minutes 15 to 16 Publication

**On screen** Open the prepared term publication preview. Verify the year, term, section and eligible records. Publish only if the rehearsed preview is ready. Otherwise show the separate prepared published sample.

**Say** “Saving marks does not immediately release them to parents. The office previews and explicitly publishes the report card. The guardian then sees the released snapshot. This gives the school control over when results become available.”

*Presenter note A term can include several assessments. Prepare all required results and remarks in advance so the preview works. Relevant later corrections can make a publication stale until the school reviews and republishes it. Do not promise totals, ranking, GPA or weighted board calculations as current features.*

# Guardian view notices and documents

## Minutes 16 to 20 Guardian Portal

**On screen** Switch to the Guardian browser profile and refresh. Open Aarav's child card, profile, attendance and published report card. Download or open the eligible report card PDF. If a second approved child exists, briefly show the child switcher.

**Say** “This is the Guardian Portal. The guardian can open only the children for whom the school has granted access. For Aarav, we can see his approved profile, submitted attendance and published results.”

**Say** “The guardian can open the released report card and download its eligible PDF. If the same guardian has access to more than one child, the child switcher keeps those records easy to navigate.”

**Say** “Teachers and guardians use their own areas. They do not receive unrestricted access to office administration.”

## Minutes 20 to 22 Notices and WhatsApp Assist

**On screen** Show a relevant portal notice. Switch to Administrator and show its audience or the message queue. Open a prepared WhatsApp link only if the contact is a safe test record. Stop before sending.

**Say** “The office can publish a notice to the whole school, a class or a section. Guardians see notices relevant to their children.”

**Say** “For eligible guardians who have opted in, WhatsApp Assist prepares the contact and message text. A staff member opens WhatsApp and completes the send, then records the outcome in the system. Automatic API sending would be a separately agreed integration.”

## Minutes 22 to 23 Supported school PDFs

**On screen** Open Documents and show the three implemented document types. Use a prepared bonafide certificate or attendance summary to avoid delaying the meeting.

**Say** “The current system supports bonafide certificates, attendance summaries and report card PDFs. Issued documents have serial numbers and stored content so the school can trace them and reprint the same record.”

*Presenter note Do not describe a staff marked message as provider confirmed delivery. Do not promise Transfer Certificate issuance or school logo printing in the current PDF letterhead. Confirm those requirements separately.*

# Timetable onboarding and meeting close

## Minutes 23 to 24 Timetable

**On screen** Open the prepared section weekly timetable. Point to a period, subject and derived teacher. Briefly open the timetable issues report.

**Say** “The office maintains the weekly timetable by section and period. The teacher follows the dated subject assignment. Known teacher clashes are checked, and the issues view helps staff review conflicts that may appear after assignment changes.”

## Minutes 24 to 25 Excel onboarding

**On screen** Open the prepared import preview with a sample row issue. Point to warnings, row problems and confirmation. Do not confirm the batch during the main demo.

**Say** “For onboarding, staff can use the Excel template. The system checks the file and shows errors or warnings before confirmation. This gives the school a chance to review the data before new records are created.”

## Minutes 25 to 26 Permissions and record history

**On screen** Point to an existing correction record, assignment history or account event using a rehearsed page. Keep temporary credentials off screen.

**Say** “Access follows roles and permissions. Important corrections, document actions and account operations leave a record. We will agree hosting, backups, support and the school's data handling responsibilities in the implementation proposal.”

## Minutes 26 to 30 Requirements and next steps

**On screen** Open the requirements questionnaire or a meeting notes page. Record answers rather than making unreviewed commitments.

**Say** “That completes the main demonstration. Which two or three school processes would you want to improve first?”

Ask, then pause for each answer:

- What do you use today, and what is hardest for your staff?

- Which report card, certificate and attendance formats must we follow?

- Are fees, payments, additional modules or integrations essential for launch?

- Who will coordinate data, approve the scope and test the system?

**Say** “We will compare your requirements with the current product and identify configuration, additional development and future modules. We will then send a written scope with costs, responsibilities and delivery dates for approval. Thank you for your time.”

# Answers to common client questions

Use these answers when the topic comes up. Record the request and return to the planned demonstration.

## Can you add our school requirements

**Say** “We can assess the changes you need. We will first confirm the workflow, review sample formats and agree the scope, cost and schedule before implementation.”

## Does it include fees and online payments

**Say** “Fees and online payments are outside the current demonstration scope. If they are required, we will document the financial workflow and propose that module separately.”

## Does WhatsApp send automatically

**Say** “The current workflow is WhatsApp Assist. Staff complete the send in WhatsApp. Automatic API delivery requires a separate provider integration and agreed operating costs.”

## Can we promote everyone to the next academic year

**Say** “Bulk academic year rollover is planned and is not part of the current implemented demo. We will confirm the required promotion, repeat and leaving rules before including it in a proposal.”

## Can parents see other children or unpublished marks

**Say** “Guardian access requires an approved relationship to the child. The portal shows eligible released report cards rather than unrestricted teacher marks entry. We will demonstrate the agreed access cases during acceptance testing.”

## Do you have a mobile app or offline use

**Say** “The School ERP is an online browser-based system and can be installed as a PWA on supported devices. The current PWA remains online-only. Native apps, offline workflows, push notifications and background sync require separate assessment and scope.”

## What is the price and how soon can we launch

**Say** “We will quote after confirming the selected modules, school size, changes, migration, hosting and support. The delivery plan will also list the data and approvals needed from the school.”

## If a page is slow or unavailable

**Say** “I will use our prepared example to explain this step and record the issue for review. We can return to it after the main walkthrough.”
