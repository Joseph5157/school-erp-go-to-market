# Everclass ERP — Common Objections and Responses

> **Draft — Internal review only.** Guidance for respectful, factual answers in school meetings. Do not promise capabilities, prices, portability terms or service levels that have not been agreed. Check product statements against the [feature catalogue](../../docs/product/feature-catalogue.md) and the [sales FAQ](../faq/sales-faq.md).

**Approach:** acknowledge the concern, give the facts, say plainly what is not decided or not available, and offer the next step. Never criticise the school's current system or a competitor.

---

### "Why pay an annual support charge?"

**Acknowledge:** It is reasonable to ask what the yearly charge is for.

**Response:** The setup fee covers getting the school started. The annual support and maintenance charge covers keeping the installation running safely after go-live: help for staff, investigating and correcting defects in delivered features, security updates and dependency upgrades, and monitoring. Software that holds children's information needs regular updates. Hosting and new development are not part of this charge; they are shown separately.

**Do not say:** specific support hours, response times or uptime — these are not decided.

---

### "Why is hosting charged separately?"

**Acknowledge:** It can look like an extra cost.

**Response:** Hosting is a third-party infrastructure cost that depends on the provider and the arrangement the school chooses: we can manage it, or the school can hold the account itself. Showing it separately makes it clear what is infrastructure and what is software support, and lets the school see the cost directly.

**Do not say:** that hosting is free, fixed for ever, or included in support.

---

### "What happens if the school grows?"

**Acknowledge:** Schools add students, sections and staff over time.

**Response:** Adding students, sections, subjects, teachers and guardian accounts is normal use of the product. If growth changes the hosting resources needed, the hosting line may change, and we would discuss that with the school in advance. Opening a second campus or school is different: the current product serves one school installation, so that would need a separate discussion.

**Do not say:** that the product is multi-school or multi-campus, or that the cost will never change.

---

### "Can the school request changes?"

**Acknowledge:** Every school works a little differently.

**Response:** Yes. Many requests are configuration and can be handled with existing settings. Requests that need new development go through a change-request process: we write a specification and estimate, and nothing is built until the school approves it in writing. Each approved change has its own acceptance check.

**Do not say:** that a requested feature will be delivered, or by when, before it is assessed.

---

### "Can our existing Excel records be imported?"

**Acknowledge:** Re-typing records is a real concern.

**Response:** Yes, for students, guardians and class/section placement, using our Excel template. Every row is checked before anything is saved, problems are listed row by row, and possible duplicates are flagged for the school to review. The school is responsible for preparing and cleaning the records it is authorised to share; we support the trial and final imports. Historical attendance, marks or other formats would need to be assessed separately.

**Do not say:** that any spreadsheet can be imported as it is, or that past years' data is included.

---

### "What happens if the internet stops working?"

**Acknowledge:** Connectivity can be unreliable.

**Response:** The product needs an internet connection; it does not currently work offline. Staff can use it on any device with a browser, including a phone on mobile data. A teacher can save attendance as a draft and submit it once the connection is back, within the business date. We recommend the school plans a short fallback procedure, such as a paper register for that period.

**Do not say:** that it works offline, syncs later automatically, or that the school will never be affected by an outage.

---

### "Does it include a mobile application?"

**Acknowledge:** Many staff and parents prefer their phones.

**Response:** There is no native Android or iOS app. The product works in the phone's browser and, on supported phones, can be added to the home screen so it opens like an app. It still needs an internet connection and does not send push notifications. A native app would be a separate product decision.

**Do not say:** that there is an app in the Play Store or App Store, or that one is coming.

---

### "Can the school move to another provider later?"

**Acknowledge:** It is sensible to think about this before starting.

**Response:** The school determines how its records are used and is responsible for providing data it is authorised to share. The written agreement will define our data-processing responsibilities, applicable rights, export arrangements and handover procedures, in line with applicable law. The related timelines and formats will be set out there too. Those exit terms are still being finalised with our legal advisers, and we will share them in writing before the school signs.

**Do not say:** a specific export format, a handover period, an unconditional data-ownership promise, that source code is transferred, or any other portability term not yet agreed.

---

### Other questions to handle with care

| Question | Short, factual answer |
|---|---|
| "Can it collect fees?" | Not today. Fees and online payments would need a separate scope. |
| "Does it send WhatsApp messages automatically?" | No. It prepares messages and staff send them from their phone; automatic sending would be a separate integration. |
| "Is it DPDP compliant?" | We do not claim automatic compliance. The product has access controls, consent records and audit history; the school should confirm its obligations with its adviser. |
| "Can we have it in Telugu?" | The interface is in English today; PDFs include Telugu fonts. |
| "When will we be live?" | After requirements are confirmed we agree stages; dates depend on data and decisions from both sides. |
| "What is your uptime guarantee?" | Service levels are not yet decided; we will state them in the agreement once approved. |
