# Privacy and outreach controls

> **Draft — Internal review only.** GTM-OUTREACH-01. These rules apply to every template, post and tracker in `sales/outreach/` and `social/`. They are internal working rules, not legal advice. The owner should confirm them with a legal adviser before outreach begins.

## The rules

| # | Rule | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No contact scraping** | Do not copy phone numbers or emails from directories, maps listings, social media profiles, school websites or WhatsApp groups into a list for outreach. Do not use scraping tools, browser extensions or purchased lists. |
| 2 | **No bulk unsolicited messages** | No broadcast lists, mass emails, automated sequences, auto-dialers or "blast" tools. Each message goes to one school, written for that school, after a person decides it is appropriate. |
| 3 | **No real prospect records in Git** | School names, people's names, phone numbers, emails, meeting notes and completed trackers are stored outside this repository, in the place the owner approves. Only blank templates live here. |
| 4 | **Respect opt-outs** | If anyone says "not interested", "do not contact" or similar, stop at once on every channel. Record only the opt-out (see [lead tracker guide](lead-tracker-guide.md)) so nobody contacts them again by mistake. |
| 5 | **Follow messaging rules** | Follow WhatsApp's business and acceptable-use policies, India's telecom rules on unsolicited commercial communication (TRAI), and the [DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf) as applicable. The owner confirms which obligations apply before outreach begins. |
| 6 | **Never share demo credentials or private staging URLs** | Do not put usernames, passwords, sign-in links or staging addresses in any message, post, email signature or attachment. Demos are run by our presenter on our device. |
| 7 | **Human review and manual authorisation** | A named person reviews and sends every message by hand. No message is generated and sent automatically. No post is scheduled or published without the owner's approval. |

## When first contact is appropriate

Contact a school only when there is a reasonable basis for it, and use the channel that fits:

| Situation | Suitable first channel |
|---|---|
| The school or contact person asked us to get in touch, or enquired | The channel they used or asked for |
| A referrer has asked the contact's permission to share their details | The channel the contact agreed to |
| We met the person at an event and they gave us their number or card | The channel they gave us |
| A school's publicly listed office line, for a business enquiry | A polite telephone call to the office, or an in-person visit during office hours |
| A school's publicly listed office email, for a business enquiry | One short, individual email |

Do **not** send a first WhatsApp message to a number we were not given for that purpose. If the only number we have is from a listing, call the office or visit instead, and ask whether the school is open to a short message.

## What every message must do

- Say who is writing, from which company (`[Company name]`), and why.
- Make no claim beyond the [feature catalogue](../../docs/product/feature-catalogue.md) and the [sales FAQ](../faq/sales-faq.md).
- Make it easy to say no. Each template has an opt-out line; keep it.
- Contain no price, discount, delivery date, hosting promise, service level or custom-development promise.
- Contain no screenshots of real or staging data, no credentials and no staging links.
- Contain no student, guardian or staff information, even fictional names, unless they come from approved fictional demo material.

## Follow-up limits

- No more than **three** messages to a school that has not replied: the first message, one follow-up and one final follow-up (see [templates](outreach-templates.md)).
- Leave at least **five working days** between unanswered messages.
- After the final follow-up, mark the lead closed and do not contact the school again unless they get in touch.
- Contact during school office hours only. Avoid exam weeks, results days and festival holidays where known.

These limits are internal working defaults for owner review, not legal thresholds.

## Personal information we may hold about a prospect

Only what is needed to continue a business conversation: the contact's name, role, school, the business channel they gave us and the date of their consent or enquiry. Do not record personal opinions, family details or anything about students. Delete a prospect's details when the conversation has ended and they are no longer needed, following the retention rule in the [lead tracker guide](lead-tracker-guide.md).
