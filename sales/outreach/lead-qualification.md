# Lead qualification guide

> **Draft — Internal review only.** GTM-OUTREACH-01. A short internal guide for deciding whether, and how, to take a school forward. It is lighter than the [requirements capture sheet](../proposals/school-requirements-capture.md), which is used once a school agrees to a requirements discussion. Record answers in a private copy of the [lead tracker](lead-tracker-guide.md), **never in Git**.

## How to use it

Ask these questions naturally across the first call, visit or demo. Do not read them out as a form. It is fine to leave some unanswered until later. Do not record anything about students, guardians or staff.

## The eight areas

### 1. School size and academic structure

- Approximate number of students, and the [segment](target-segments.md): small (300–500), medium (501–1,000) or larger (1,001–2,000)
- Grades offered, number of sections, board or affiliation
- Single campus or more than one

*Fit note:* the current product serves one school installation. More than one campus needs a separate discussion; do not promise it.

### 2. Existing software or manual processes

- Paper registers, spreadsheets, other software, or a mix
- What works well today. Do not criticise the current system.
- Any contract or renewal date the school mentions (record the month only)

### 3. Most urgent problems

- "What takes the most time for your office?"
- "Which process causes the most phone calls or follow-up?"
- Record the school's own words, up to three items.

### 4. Required versus optional modules

Map each need to the catalogue areas (M01–M12) and mark it **Must have** or **Later**.

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Current feature | In the [feature catalogue](../../docs/product/feature-catalogue.md); still to be shown on the deployed build |
| Configuration | Supported through existing settings |
| Separate assessment | Listed in the catalogue's "Current boundaries and future requests", or needs new development |

If a must-have need is a separate assessment item (for example fee collection, transfer certificates or automatic SMS), say so plainly and record it. That is not a reason to drop the lead, but it changes the next step.

### 5. Availability of a school decision-maker

- Who makes the final decision: owner, correspondent, management committee or principal?
- Has that person been part of the conversation?
- Who would evaluate day to day: principal, administrator, academic coordinator?

### 6. Data migration requirements

- Where student and guardian records are kept today, and in what format
- Who at the school would prepare and clean the data
- Whether historical attendance or marks are expected (these need assessment; standard Excel import covers students, guardians and class/section placement)

Do **not** ask for or accept real data files at this stage.

### 7. Demo interest

- Not yet / short (about 10 minutes) / detailed (around 30 minutes)
- Who should attend
- The two or three processes they most want to see

### 8. Next action

Every lead has exactly one next action, a date and an internal owner. Use the stages in the [sales process](sales-process.md).

## Simple qualification rating

Use this to prioritise time, not to judge schools.

| Rating | Typical signals | Usual next step |
|---|---|---|
| **Ready** | Decision-maker involved; clear problems that match current features; open to a requirements discussion | Requirements discussion, then proposal preparation |
| **Developing** | Interest shown, but decision-maker not yet involved, or priorities unclear | Demo with the decision-maker present |
| **Early** | Curious, no current need, or main needs are outside current scope | One polite follow-up at an agreed time, or close |
| **Closed** | Not interested, opted out, or no reply after the final follow-up | No further contact unless the school gets in touch |

## Things to avoid

- Promising a feature, price, date, hosting arrangement or service level to "qualify" a school
- Asking for budget figures before the school has seen a demo; the budget question in the requirements sheet is optional
- Recording personal opinions about the people involved
- Storing any of this in Git, shared chat groups or unapproved tools
