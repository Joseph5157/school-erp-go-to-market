# School outreach and lead generation kit

> **Draft — Internal review only.** GTM-OUTREACH-01, 2026-11-10. Nothing here has been sent, published or scheduled. Every message is a draft for a person to review, adapt and send by hand, one school at a time.

This kit helps the team introduce the product to private schools in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh (around 300–2,000 students). It sits between first contact and the existing commercial package in [`../proposals/`](../proposals/).

## Read these first

1. [Privacy and outreach controls](privacy-and-outreach-controls.md). These rules apply to every file below.
2. [Target-school segments](target-segments.md). Who we approach and what matters to each size of school.

## Placeholders

The product name is decided: **Everclass ERP** (owner, 2026-11-15). Company name, logo, phone, email and website are **not approved**. Templates use these placeholders. Do not replace them with invented values:

| Placeholder | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[Company name]` | Our company, once approved |
| `[Your name]`, `[Your role]` | The person sending the message |
| `[Your phone]`, `[Your email]` | Approved business contact details |
| `[School name]`, `[Contact name]`, `[Contact role]` | The school and person, filled in at the moment of sending, never in Git |
| `[Referrer name]` | The person who introduced us, only with their permission |
| `[Date]`, `[Time]`, `[Day]` | Meeting or follow-up details |

## Not covered by this kit

- Prices, discounts, hosting arrangements, delivery dates, support hours, service levels and custom development. None of these is approved; see the [pricing framework](../pricing/pricing-framework.md) and its open decisions.
- CRM software or any integration with WhatsApp, email or telephone systems.
- Real school names, contact details or prospect records. Those stay outside this repository.
