# Everclass ERP — Client Presentation Speaker Notes

**Status: Draft.** A **10-minute conversation opener**, not a live-demo rehearsal. Use plain language. Do not show real school data or unapproved commercial promises.

| Slide | Suggested time | Presenter cue |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Everclass ERP | 0:30 | Frame this as a discussion about the school's current processes, not a feature sales pitch. |
| 2. Fragmented school work | 0:45 | Ask which record or approval currently causes the most follow-up work. Do not claim quantified savings. |
| 3. Student journey | 0:50 | Trace a fictional applicant into attendance, assessments and released results; describe the actual school review points. |
| 4. Product areas | 0:45 | Summarize the four diagram groups; say the full catalogue has 12 documented areas. |
| 5. Administrator | 0:55 | Emphasize configuration, admissions, review, publication and auditability. |
| 6. Teacher | 0:55 | Desktop menu is **My day**; mobile menu is **Today**. Access depends on authorized current assignments and academic year. |
| 7. Guardian | 0:55 | Explain child-specific access and published reports only; no unrestricted teacher marks. |
| 8. Results publication | 0:50 | Describe teacher marks → school finalization → explicit publication → guardian visibility. |
| 9. Daily operations | 0:55 | Describe one audience per notice (whole school, one class, or one section). WhatsApp Assist is staff-completed, not automatic delivery. |
| 10. Current boundaries | 1:05 | Be transparent when fees/payments or other not-yet-built modules are requested. Record them for a separate scope assessment. |
| 11. Implementation | 0:55 | Customized installation, setup fee plus annual support are the preferred commercial structure. Do not quote figures or launch dates before discovery. |
| 12. Questions and next steps | 1:00 | Ask for the school's top three needs, its report-card formats and the person authorized to approve scope. |

**Total target:** approximately 10 minutes, plus questions. Shorten slides 4 and 9 if the audience wants an 8-minute overview.
